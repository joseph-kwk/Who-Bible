🔄 Clear Browser Cache

Having trouble seeing the latest updates? Let's clear your browser cache!

Manual Cache Clear Instructions:

  1. Chrome/Edge: Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac)
  2. Firefox: Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + Delete (Mac)
  3. Safari: Press Cmd + Option + E, then reload
  4. Select "Cached images and files" and click "Clear data"
  5. Close and reopen your browser
